### A07: OKR Cascader

**Key Findings**
- Company Objective (from GIVEN goal): "Become the default scheduling tool for independent dental clinics in UAE and KSA", with the top Key Result of 300 paying clinics by Oct 2027 (GIVEN). With only 3 people (GIVEN), "departments" collapse to three owners: Founder (Sales/CS), Engineer 1 (Product/Core), Engineer 2 (Platform/Integrations/Localisation). Each owner can credibly carry at most 3 KRs per quarter (Doerr guidance, ASSUMED cap). Confidence: High.
- The single-number company KR is a lagging indicator. Grove's output/indicator pairing requires a paired counter-metric: logo retention, otherwise the founder is rewarded for closing clinics that churn. Confidence: High.
- Quarterly cascade arithmetic (ASSUMED back-loaded ramp, product live month 3): Q1 0-10 clinics, Q2 ~50, Q3 ~140, Q4 ~300 cumulative. Q4 alone requires ~160 net adds (~53/month), which is a 3-4x step-up over Q2 run-rate. Without a capacity change at Q3, the Q4 KR is unreachable; scoring at 0.5 (~150 clinics) is the likely grade. Confidence: Medium.
- MRR KR derived: 300 x 79 USD = 23,700 USD MRR (GIVEN arithmetic); at 150 clinics, 11,850 USD MRR.

**Critical Assumptions**
- GIVEN: 300 clinics, 12 months, 79 USD/month, 3-person team, 250,000 USD.
- ASSUMED: 2% monthly logo churn; ~30% of trialing clinics convert to paid; time-to-first-booking under 7 days is achievable with assisted onboarding; WhatsApp is the dominant patient-reminder channel in the region (to be confirmed via clinic interviews / Meta regional data).

**Recommendations**
1. Q1 (Nov 2026-Jan 2027) Objective "Prove clinics will switch". KRs: Founder: 25 discovery interviews and 10 signed pilot LOIs; Eng 1: booking engine + multi-dentist calendar in production by 31 Jan; Eng 2: Arabic/English RTL UI and WhatsApp/SMS reminders shipped to pilots.
2. Q2 (Feb-Apr 2027) Objective "Convert pilots to revenue". KRs: 50 paying clinics; 90% of paid clinics book 50+ appointments in trailing 30 days; median onboarding time under 3 hours; billing live with automated card/bank collection.
3. Q3 (May-Jul 2027) Objective "Build a repeatable channel". KRs: 140 paying clinics; 70 demos/month; 25% of new clinics from referral or self-serve; churn at or below 2%/month; KSA share at least 30% of new adds.
4. Q4 (Aug-Oct 2027) Objective "Scale". KRs: 300 paying (stretch, 0.7 grade = 210 acceptable); 23,700 USD MRR stretch; net revenue retention 100%+; support tickets per clinic per month under 1.5.
5. Run monthly OKR check-ins with a confidence score (1-10) per KR; any KR at confidence 3 or below for two consecutive months triggers re-planning rather than silent slippage.
