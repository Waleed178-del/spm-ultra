### A27: GTM Channels Strategist

**Key Findings** (bulleted, quantified)
- Capacity gap (High confidence). Reaching 300 paying clinics (GIVEN) in 12 months with an ASSUMED ~2.5% monthly logo churn needs ~330-350 gross wins. If the product is sellable from month 3 (ASSUMED, given 2 engineers starting at MVP stage), there are 9 selling months, so ~37-39 wins per month. A single founder in field sales can close an ASSUMED 8-15 clinics per month at steady state. Basis: ~10-12 demos per week, 30-40% demo-to-close, plus time lost to onboarding and support. Founder-led direct sales therefore reaches ~90-140 clinics in 12 months, not 300.
- Channel ranking (Bullseye test, ASSUMED conversion figures):
  1. **Founder field and WhatsApp outbound** (UAE, city-dense). ~1,500-clinic list, 25-35% reply rate to warm WhatsApp or walk-in, 30-40% demo-to-close. Expected yield: 90-140 clinics. CAC driver: founder time. (High confidence it works; Medium on volume.)
  2. **Dental supply and equipment distributor partnerships.** Distributors' sales reps already visit clinics monthly. One or two partners with 5-15 reps each, at 0.5-1 close per rep per month, gives 30-80 clinics over months 5-12. Cost: an ASSUMED 20-30% recurring revenue share, or a 100-150 USD bounty per clinic. (Medium confidence.)
  3. **Clinic-to-clinic referral.** Dentists know each other through study clubs and shared suppliers. At an ASSUMED 0.15-0.25 referrals per active clinic per year, this gives ~20-40 clinics. (Medium confidence.)
  4. **Dental events.** AEEDC Dubai (ASSUMED to be held around February each year) and the Saudi dental society conferences. One booth at an ASSUMED 8,000-15,000 USD, generating 150-300 leads and 15-30 closes. (Low-Medium confidence.)
  5. **Paid search and social** (Google, Instagram) aimed at owner-dentists. An ASSUMED 400-900 USD paid CAC per clinic at low intent. Deprioritise until conversion is proven. (Low confidence.)
- A blended plan of channels 1+2+3+4 yields ~155-290 gross wins. 300 net is reachable only at the top of every range. (Medium confidence)
- Saudi Arabia requires an Arabic-speaking on-the-ground seller or partner. The founder's capacity cannot cover two countries in person.

**Critical Assumptions** (bulleted, GIVEN vs ASSUMED)
- GIVEN: 1 founder who sells; 2 engineers; 250,000 USD seed; 79 USD per month; target of 300 clinics in 12 months.
- ASSUMED: the product is sellable at month 3; churn is 2.5% per month; and the conversion rates above, based on typical SMB vertical SaaS field motions. These would be confirmed by the first 60 days of pipeline data.
- ASSUMED: distributors (for example regional dental supply houses) will carry a software line for a revenue share. Unvalidated.
- ASSUMED: dental event timing and costs. Confirm with the organisers.

**Recommendations** (numbered, actionable)
1. In months 0-3, the founder runs 30 discovery-plus-design-partner clinics in Dubai and Sharjah. Convert 15-20 of them to paid at month 3.
2. Sign one UAE dental distributor partnership by month 5. Offer a 25% recurring share for 12 months, capped, plus co-branded demo kits.
3. Hire or contract one Arabic-speaking sales rep on commission (ASSUMED 2,500-3,500 USD per month base plus 100 USD per close) by month 5. This roughly doubles direct capacity.
4. Book one AEEDC presence (a booth or a co-exhibit with the distributor) for the Feb 2027 edition (ASSUMED date). Pre-book 50 demos.
5. Launch the referral mechanic at month 4: one free month for both clinics.
6. Hold paid ads at no more than 1,000 USD per month until demo-to-close is at least 30%.
7. Weekly channel scorecard: leads, demos, closes and CAC per channel. Kill any channel with fewer than 3 closes after 60 days.
