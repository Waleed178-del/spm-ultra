### A09: Resource Allocation Modeler

**Key Findings**
- Headcount is 3 (GIVEN) with no sales, support, onboarding, design or QA staff. Every non-engineering hour falls on the founder. Confidence: High.
- Founder load at 300 clinics (ASSUMED unit times): selling at ~37 gross adds/month and 20% demo conversion = ~185 demos/month x 1.5 h incl. travel/prep = ~280 h/month; onboarding at 3 h/clinic x 37 = ~110 h/month; customer support at 0.5 h/clinic/month x 200 average live clinics = ~100 h/month; admin/fundraising ~30 h/month. Total ~520 h/month vs a sustainable ~200 h/month (ASSUMED, 50 h/week). Load is ~2.6x capacity: severe burnout risk. Confidence: Medium.
- At a Base plan of ~150 clinics (~17 gross adds/month): ~85 demos (~130 h), onboarding ~50 h, support ~40 h, admin ~30 h = ~250 h/month, still 125% of capacity from month 7. Even the Base plan needs one extra hire by month 6. Confidence: Medium.
- Engineers: ~120 focused h/sprint combined (ASSUMED). Phase 1 (months 1-3) 100% build. Phase 2 (months 4-6) ~75% build / 25% support. Phase 3 (months 7-12) ~60/40 as clinic count passes ~100. Both engineers are single points of knowledge for their areas.
- Cost of relief (ASSUMED GCC rates): onboarding/support specialist ~2,500-3,500 USD/month loaded; Arabic-speaking SDR/AE ~4,000-6,000 USD/month incl. commission; 6 months of one each from month 5 = ~40,000-57,000 USD, i.e. 16-23% of the 250,000 USD budget (GIVEN).
- Burn baseline (ASSUMED): 2 engineers ~7,000 USD/month each loaded, founder 4,000, infra/tools 1,500, travel KSA/UAE 2,000, marketing 2,500 = ~24,000 USD/month = ~10.4 months runway on 250,000 USD before any revenue. Adding two hires cuts runway to ~8 months unless revenue offsets. Confidence: Medium.

**Critical Assumptions**
- GIVEN: 2 engineers, 1 founder-seller, 250,000 USD, 300-clinic goal, 79 USD/month.
- ASSUMED: salaries at UAE-based mid-level rates (offshore engineers would be ~40-50% lower); 20% demo conversion; 3 h onboarding per clinic; 0.5 h support per clinic per month; founder sustainable at 50 h/week. Confirm via regional salary surveys (e.g. recruiter benchmarks for Dubai/Riyadh).

**Recommendations**
1. Hire a bilingual (Arabic/English) onboarding-and-support specialist by month 5 (Mar 2027); this frees ~150 h/month of founder time for selling.
2. Hire one commission-heavy Arabic-speaking seller based in Riyadh by month 6 if Q2 shows 40+ paid clinics; otherwise KSA stays a pilot market.
3. Cap founder in-person demos at 4/day; route the rest to 20-minute video demos to roughly double demo throughput.
4. Rotate engineers on a weekly on-call schedule from month 4 so build sprints keep a protected 75%.
5. Track founder hours weekly; above 60 h for 3 consecutive weeks triggers a scope or target cut.
