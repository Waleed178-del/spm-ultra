### A10: Change Management Planner

**Key Findings**
- The adopting unit is not the clinic owner who signs; it is the receptionist/front-desk staff (ASSUMED 1-3 per independent clinic) who currently book by phone, WhatsApp and paper diary or Excel (ASSUMED incumbent workflow). If the receptionist does not adopt within ~14 days, the clinic churns at renewal. Confidence: Medium.
- ADKAR friction map (ASSUMED, to be validated in pilot interviews):
  - Awareness: owners feel no-shows but rarely quantify them; ASSUMED no-show rate 15-25% of appointments in the region is the core pain to quantify.
  - Desire: receptionists may see the tool as surveillance or extra work; double-entry with an existing PMS kills desire.
  - Knowledge: mixed language preference; many front-desk staff in UAE are non-Arabic expatriates (e.g. Filipino, South Asian) while KSA staff are predominantly Arabic-speaking (ASSUMED). Bilingual UI and training are mandatory, not optional.
  - Ability: data migration of existing future bookings is the single biggest switching barrier; a clinic with 4-8 weeks of forward bookings cannot cut over in one day.
  - Reinforcement: patients must also change, by replying to WhatsApp confirmations rather than calling.
- Training load (ASSUMED): 3 h initial onboarding + 1 h follow-up at day 7 per clinic. At 300 clinics = ~1,200 h; at 150 clinics = ~600 h. This cannot be delivered 1:1 by a founder who also sells. Confidence: Medium.
- Turnover of receptionists (ASSUMED 30-50% annual in independent clinics) means retraining recurs; ~1 retraining event per clinic per year.

**Critical Assumptions**
- GIVEN: independent non-chain dental clinics, UAE and KSA, 3-person team, 79 USD/month.
- ASSUMED: incumbent workflow is phone/WhatsApp + paper/Excel or a basic PMS; owner is the economic buyer, receptionist is the daily user; no-show rate 15-25%; receptionist turnover 30-50%/year. Confirm via 25 clinic interviews and pilot usage logs.

**Recommendations**
1. Kotter step 1 (urgency): give every prospect a no-show cost calculator (no-shows x average visit value) during the demo; target a documented monthly loss figure for each signed clinic.
2. Run a 14-day parallel-run cutover: paper/Excel continues for existing bookings, all new bookings go into the product; migrate forward bookings via assisted CSV import in week 2.
3. Build a 3-asset bilingual training kit before paid launch (Feb 2027): 5-minute Arabic and English video walkthroughs, a one-page laminated front-desk card, and an in-app checklist. Goal: cut live onboarding from 3 h to 1 h by month 6.
4. Appoint a "front-desk champion" per clinic; give them a visible win (daily confirmed-appointments view) and a small recognition (e.g. certificate), not the owner's audit dashboard.
5. Define adoption health: 80%+ of appointments created in-product by day 14 and 60%+ patient confirmation reply rate by day 30; clinics below threshold get a proactive call.
6. Treat receptionist turnover as an event: send self-serve retraining automatically when a new staff user is added.

**Cross-Dependencies**
- [A09] Requires the bilingual onboarding specialist by month 5; without it the 600-1,200 h training load lands on the founder.
- [A23] CROSS-DEPENDENCY: receptionist UX must allow booking in 3 taps or under 30 seconds, otherwise phone-and-paper wins.
- [A17] CROSS-DEPENDENCY: feed "receptionist non-adoption leading to churn" as a High-probability / High-impact risk, and "forward-booking migration failure" as Medium/High.
- [A21] CROSS-DEPENDENCY: CSV import and bilingual templates are adoption-critical and must remain in MVP.
