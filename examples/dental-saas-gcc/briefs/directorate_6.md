## Directorate 6 Brief: Commercialization & GTM Execution (Agents 26-30)

**Directorate Verdict**

The unit economics work. The 12-month volume target does not work with the team as given.

- **Unit economics:** at 79 USD per month (GIVEN), the base-case LTV:CAC is about 4x and CAC payback is 7-12 months [A29].
- **Volume:** a founder selling alone closes about 8-15 clinics per month, which gives about 90-140 clinics in 12 months [A27]. With one added seller, a distributor partner, referrals and one dental event, the base case is about 180-230 net paying clinics. The 300 target (GIVEN) is reachable only if every channel lands at the top of its range.
- **Cash:** the second constraint is cash. Acquiring about 330 gross clinics consumes most of the 250,000 USD seed (GIVEN) unless clinics prepay annually [A29].
- **Confidence:** Medium on the economics. High that 300 clinics needs more than the 3 people given.

**Consolidated Findings**

1. **ICP** [A26]
   - Independent clinics with 2-5 chairs and 1-4 dentists, booking 25-60 appointments a day by phone and WhatsApp, with no patient-facing booking tool.
   - Beachhead: Dubai and Sharjah for months 0-6. Riyadh only after 100 or more paying UAE clinics.
   - The owner-dentist is the buyer. The receptionist is the daily user and the champion.
   - Leads are qualified on 4 behavioural markers, and only those with 3 or more get a demo.
   - Addressable base: about 6,000-9,000 independent clinics (ASSUMED), so 300 clinics is about 3-5% penetration. (Medium)
2. **The core job is no-show recovery, not scheduling** [A26][A28]
   - ASSUMED 15-25% no-shows, worth about 1,000-4,800 USD per month lost per clinic.
   - Category: "WhatsApp-first no-show prevention for dental clinics, Arabic and English."
   - Counter-positions: no per-booking commission (against marketplaces); works alongside the existing PMS (against PMS suites).
   - Rep line: "One saved appointment pays for the month." (Medium)
3. **Channels, in priority order** [A27]
   1. Founder field sales plus WhatsApp outbound to a list of about 1,500 UAE clinics.
   2. A dental-distributor partnership by month 5.
   3. Clinic-to-clinic referral.
   4. AEEDC Dubai (ASSUMED Feb 2027), shared with the distributor.
   5. Paid digital, capped at 1,000 USD per month until demo-to-close is at least 30%.

   Any channel with fewer than 3 closes after 60 days is cut. (Medium)
4. **Unit economics** [A29]
   - Contribution of about 62 USD per clinic per month after WhatsApp message costs (gross margin 75-82%, ASSUMED).
   - LTV of about 2,480 USD at 2.5% monthly churn.
   - Blended CAC of 450-750 USD.
   - Payback is the binding metric, with spend gated at 12 months or less. (Medium)
5. **Retention** [A30]
   - Biggest churn drivers: receptionist turnover (30-40%) and failure to activate in the first 14 days (25-35%).
   - Fixes: a 14-day activation standard (at least 80% of clinics); a morning WhatsApp summary as the hook trigger; a monthly ROI report to the owner; multi-user seats; dunning (automatic card retry and payment reminders).
   - Clinics that activate are ASSUMED to churn at about 1.5% per month, against 4% or more for those that do not. (Low-Medium)
6. **Pricing and cash levers** [A28][A29][A30]
   - Launch at 79 USD (GIVEN).
   - Offer annual prepay at 790 USD, targeting a 30-40% annual mix. This adds about 60,000-90,000 USD of year-1 cash and lowers churn.
   - Test 99 USD for new cohorts from month 4, which raises LTV by about 25%.
   - Pass through or cap message costs above 600 messages per clinic per month.

**Internal Conflicts Resolved**

1. **Base-case volume.** [A27]'s channel sum gives 155-290 gross wins. [A29] and [A30] model the full 300.
   - Resolution: the base case is 180-230 net clinics, and 300 is the stretch case.
   - Why [A27] wins: it is the only bottom-up capacity estimate. The others take 300 as an input.
2. **Distributor compensation.** [A27] proposes a 25% revenue share, capped at 12 months. [A29] objects to a perpetual 25% share, which cuts LTV by 25%.
   - Resolution: accept either a 12-month capped share (about 237 USD per clinic) or a 100-150 USD bounty. A perpetual share is rejected.
   - Why: both acceptable options cost less than 40% of blended CAC.
3. **GTM spend.** [A27] estimates 60,000-90,000 USD. [A29]'s CAC × 330 gives about 198,000 USD.
   - Resolution: [A29]'s figure includes the imputed cost of the founder's time (about 60,000 USD), which belongs to D3's salary line.
   - Incremental cash GTM: 90,000 USD planned, with 120,000 USD as a hard cap [A29].
4. **Geography.** [A26] says UAE only until 100 clinics. [A27] wants an Arabic-speaking rep by month 5.
   - Resolution: hire the rep in month 5 and deploy them in the UAE first. They open Riyadh only when the 100-clinic gate is met.
   - Why: city density protects close rates.
5. **Referral yield.** [A27] estimates 20-40 clinics; [A30] estimates 15-35.
   - Resolution: use [A30]'s 15-35, the more conservative figure. [A30] owns the mechanism.
6. **Price.** [A28] and [A29] argue 79 USD under-captures value.
   - Resolution: launch at the GIVEN 79 USD and test 99 USD from month 4. The price is not changed before there is pilot proof.
